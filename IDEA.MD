
POCKET FM HACKATHON
AI Creator Copilot for Content Production
Product Overview
The AI Creator Copilot is an end-to-end storytelling assistant that helps creators turn a simple story idea into a complete, publish-ready audio series. Instead of replacing the creator, the AI works as a collaborative partner throughout the entire content creation process. At every stage, the creator can review, edit, approve, or regenerate the generated content, ensuring complete creative control.
The goal is to reduce the time required to create a high-quality series from weeks to minutes while maintaining consistency across the story, characters, and episodes.

Complete Workflow
Step 1: Story Idea Input
The creator begins by providing all the initial details about the story.
This includes information such as:
Complete story idea (detailed description)
Genre
Theme
Tone
Language
Any additional notes
The more details the creator provides, the better the AI understands the vision.

Step 2: AI Clarification
After understanding the initial story idea, the AI checks if anything is unclear or missing.
Instead of making assumptions, it asks the creator up to 5 clarification questions.
Along with question it will include options and a text input:
Multiple-choice options
Text input
Combination of both
For example:
Choose one of the following, or describe your own idea:
A. Born Different (Recommended)
The protagonist has always possessed these abilities but never fully understood them. The story begins as they gradually uncover the true extent and origin of their powers.
B. Triggered by a Major Event
An accident, near-death experience, betrayal, or traumatic event suddenly awakens dormant abilities, forcing the protagonist into a completely different life.
C. Trained or Gifted
The powers are intentionally granted or taught by another person, organization, ancient artifact, or mysterious force. Mastering them becomes part of the journey.
D.  write your own idea
Should the story have a happy or tragic ending?
Should romance be the main focus or a side plot?
Does the main character already know about their powers?
This helps the AI fully understand the creator's vision before generating the story.

Step 3: Series Blueprint Generation
Once all required information is collected, the AI generates the complete blueprint for the series.
This includes:
Overall story plot/ Story world / Main storyline
Characters / Character descriptions / Character relationships
Tone of the series
Theme
This serves as the foundation for the entire series.
The creator can:
Edit any section
Regenerate specific parts
Modify the plot
Approve the blueprint
Nothing moves forward until the creator is satisfied.

Step 4: Episode Planning
After approving the series blueprint, the creator specifies:
Total number of episodes
Average duration of each episode
The AI then divides the complete story into episodes.
For every episode, the AI generates:
Episode title
Episode summary
Main events
Emotional focus
Ending cliffhanger
Each episode is planned in a way that keeps listeners engaged and encourages them to continue listening.
Again, the creator can:
Edit episode summaries
Rearrange episode order
Change pacing
Add or remove events
Approve the episode plan

Step 5: Script Generation
Once the episode plan is approved, the AI generates the complete script for every episode.
The script includes:
Narration - Scene transitions, Narrator dialogues (if required), Scene setting
Character dialogues , Conversations between characters
The script follows the approved episode outline while maintaining consistency in character personalities and story progression.
The creator can edit every dialogue before moving to production.

Step 6: Voice Selection
After the script is finalized, the creator selects voices for every character.
This includes:
Narrator voice
Main character voices
Supporting character voices
Different voices can be assigned to different characters to make the audiobook feel natural and immersive.

Step 7: Audio Generation
Using Text-to-Speech, the AI generates the complete audiobook.
Each character speaks using their assigned voice.
The narration and character dialogues are automatically combined into a seamless listening experience.

Step 8: Automatic Background Music and Sound Effects
To make the audiobook more immersive, the system includes a library of pre-built background music and sound effects.
The AI automatically chooses appropriate sounds based on the current scene.
Examples include:
Romantic scenes → soft romantic background music
Horror scenes → suspenseful background music
Thunderstorm → thunder sound effects
Rain → rainfall sounds
Footsteps → walking sounds
Door opening → door sound
Battle scenes → action music
Forest scenes → birds and ambient nature sounds
Emotional scenes → soft emotional background music
These sounds are subtle and enhance the listening experience without overpowering the narration.
The creator can preview, change, or remove any automatically selected music or sound effects.

Step 9: Video Generation
After the audiobook is generated, the AI creates a video version of the episode.
Using the story, dialogues, and scene descriptions, the AI generates visuals that match the narration.
The generated video follows the progression of the episode and synchronizes with the generated audio.
This provides creators with an additional visual version of their content.

Continuing the Story
For example Initially, the AI generates the first 6 episodes of the series.
After these episodes are completed, the creator can continue building the story instead of starting from scratch.
The creator can:
Add a new plot
Introduce new characters
Add new story arcs
Continue existing storylines
Extend the series
The AI remembers everything generated previously, including:
Story events
Characters
Relationships
Tone
Themes
Previous episodes
Using this context, it generates the next set of episodes while maintaining continuity throughout the series.
This allows creators to build long-running series without losing consistency.

Creator Control
At every stage of the workflow, the creator remains in complete control.
The creator can:
Edit generated content
Regenerate specific sections
Approve each stage before moving ahead
Modify characters
Update storylines
Change dialogues
Change voices
Modify music and sound effects
The AI assists the creator throughout the process but never removes creative control.

End-to-End Workflow
Creator enters the complete story idea and preferences.
AI asks up to 5 clarification questions if required.
AI generates the complete series blueprint, including plot, characters, themes, and story direction.
Creator reviews, edits, and approves the blueprint.
Creator specifies the number of episodes and average episode length.
AI divides the story into episodes with summaries, emotional beats, and cliffhangers.
Creator reviews and approves the episode plan.
AI generates complete scripts with narration and character dialogues.
Creator edits and approves the scripts.
Creator selects voices for every character.
AI generates a multi-character audiobook using Text-to-Speech.
AI automatically adds suitable background music and sound effects from a pre-built library.
AI generates a synchronized video version of the episode.
After the first 6 episodes, the creator can continue expanding the story by adding new plots, characters, and story arcs, while the AI maintains continuity with everything created so far.

Goal
The goal of the AI Creator Copilot is to help creators transform a single story idea into a complete, high-quality, publish-ready series with minimal effort. By combining AI-assisted writing, planning, voice generation, sound design, and video creation into one workflow, creators can produce engaging long-form content much faster while still retaining full creative control over every stage of the process.
