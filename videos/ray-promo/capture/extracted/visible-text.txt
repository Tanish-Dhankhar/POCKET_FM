# Ray — Pocket FM's AI Creator Copilot

Tagline: Let creators create. Let Ray do the rest.

## The problem
Creators do not lack ideas. They lack time and a production team.
Story development → Episode planning → Scriptwriting → Casting → Voice production → Sound design.
Creativity becomes coordination.

## The solution
Ray turns one idea into a series listeners want to finish.
The creator brings: imagination, emotion, taste, final say.
Ray handles: planning, consistency, production, evaluation, continuation.
Ray removes repetitive work — not creative control.

## The flow (as shown in product UI)
1. INPUT — Write the idea or record it naturally ("I want to start a new story by… Write / Speak").
2. UNDERSTAND — Ray extracts the premise and asks four tailored questions (Question 1/4 modal).
3. BUILD — Series shape confirm ("Here's the shape of your series") → IDEA BOARD: plot & structure, genre radar, theme bars, characters, 6-episode plan.
4. PLAN — Episode arcs with hooks and cliffhangers ("The Last Light", "Frequency of Hope", "The Siege Begins"…).
5. PRODUCE — Generate Episode → "GENERATING YOUR EPISODE": threading fate, injecting plot twists, synthesizing the voices. Cinematic multi-voice audio.
6. REVIEW — Edit, regenerate, evaluate (AI Evaluator Judge), remix, approve.
7. CONTINUE — Add a new plot; Ray updates the story and creates the next episodes.

## Differentiators
- Full creative control — review, edit, regenerate, approve at every stage.
- Cliffhanger-first episodes — every episode ends with a reason to play the next one.
- AI Evaluator Judge — checks hook, pacing, character voices, escalation, clarity, ending.
- State-of-the-art audio — character voices, emotion, pauses, overlap, interruption, cinematic mix.
- Fluid series consistency — world, characters, relationships, voices, past events stay connected.
- Story extension with memory — add a plot, Ray creates the next connected episodes.
- Fast remix — reuse the original voices; change the direction without another TTS run.
- 30 voices · cinematic Audio Director · durable continuity memory · 131 automated tests.
